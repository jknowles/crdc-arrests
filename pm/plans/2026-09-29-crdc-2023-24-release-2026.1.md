# Plan: CRDC 2023-24, NYC, and API vintages (release 2026.1)

## Context

OCR released the 2023-24 CRDC in September 2026. It is on the share with the User's
Manual, the Appendix Workbook and the Master List of Definitions; the plan was checked
against all three, plus both years' school forms.

The pipeline publishes release `2025.1` covering 2015-16, 2017-18 and 2021-22. Release
`2026.1` adds 2023-24 and makes three corrections that reach back into earlier waves:

1. **NYC enters the models** at geographic-district scale, with a citywide aggregate.
   It is currently missing from every published wave.
2. **Action-plan (`-5`) and force-certified (`-6`) codes become missing** instead of
   zeros.
3. **The API serves both vintages** through a `release` parameter, so `2025.1` stays
   citable.

Imputing the missing data is set aside for its own design session once this pipeline is
built. No code has been changed yet.

## What the documentation and data establish

**2023-24 pools cleanly with the earlier waves.**
- The ARRS definitions are word for word the same in the 2021-22 and 2023-24 school
  forms.
- Privacy protection is the same ±1 perturbation with swapping, and true zeros are
  protected (Manual §5.2).
- Students arrested nationally: 34,846 in 2021-22, 33,910 in 2023-24.
- Coverage is 97,094 schools, 98.94% certified.

**`-9` is more common, and the existing handling is correct.**
- `-9` and the tool's NA autofill are documented in both waves.
- The instance items ARRS-1 and ARRS-4 went from optional to required (Appendix B). A
  zero entered there now autofills the per-student cells with `-9`.
- The pipeline's `-9` → 0 reading is right in both years.
- 10 schools, with 74 arrest instances, report instances but have every cell blank. They
  are negligible and will be logged.

**Reserve codes (Manual Table 1).**
- `-10` is suppression under EO 14168. It covers every nonbinary `_X` cell, which the
  pipeline already drops.
- `-11` (data-quality suppression) appears in neither file the pipeline reads.
- `-12` no longer exists.

**NYC.**
- CRDC files NYC as one LEA, `3620580`. CCD lists its schools under about 32
  geographic-district LEAIDs, and CCD's own `3620580` is "NYC Chancellor's Office", with
  zero schools.
- `intersect_crdc_ccd()` inner-joins COMBOKEY to `ncessch`, so NYC's roughly 1,590
  schools drop out of every wave. The 2021-22 staged data has 3,194 NY schools out of
  4,893.
- Appendix K crosswalks exist for 2021-22 and 2023-24. The 2021-22 one was there all
  along and simply unused.

NYC's arrest data by wave:

| Wave | NYC arrest data |
|---|---|
| 2015-16 | all `-5` (action plan) |
| 2017-18 | 1,562 of 1,603 schools `-6` (force certified) |
| 2021-22 | reported: 223 arrests |
| 2023-24 | reported: 137 arrests; most schools `-9`, meaning zero instances |

With `-5`/`-6` treated as missing, NYC appears in the models for 2021-22 and 2023-24 only.

**`-5`/`-6` today.** Action-plan and force-certified schools currently count as
zero-arrest schools: 157 schools in 2023-24, in 5 LEAs including Lewisville ISD, and 446
in 2021-22.

## Settled decisions

- **NYC** is modeled at CCD geographic-district scale. Citywide figures are a draw-wise
  aggregate.
- **The crosswalk** applies to all four waves, and the docs say so.
- **`-5`/`-6` are missing in every model.** Those rows are dropped, never zeroed, and
  nothing is imputed.
  - NYC in 2015-16 and 2017-18 is therefore absent, not estimated.
  - Imputation is its own later design session:
    [#6](https://gitea.civilytics.org/jared/crdc-arrests/issues/6). It records
    the questions this scoping raised:
    - missing-at-random for whole LEA-years;
    - missing covariates in m4/m5;
    - partial LEAs;
    - how to label imputed estimates.
- **Labels.** "Three-year" becomes "pooled".
- **API.** A request with no `release` gets the latest vintage.
- **Compute.** The modeling phase runs on euler.

## Steps

### Phase A: ingest and prep (efron)

**A1. 2023-24 ingest and year plumbing (`_targets.R`, `R/funs.R`)**
- `_targets.R:116-131`: add the row `"23-24"`, `y2324`, `ccd_year = 2023`.
  - Copy the CSVs and both Appendix Workbooks into `tmp/data/`. `tmp/` is a Nextcloud
    link, so they reach euler on their own.
  - CCD 2023 is live on the Urban Institute API.
- `_targets.R:229-244, 631, 688-711`: add `_y2324` to `combined_model_data`,
  `combined_sch_data`, `combined_dist_geo` and the `stage_crdc` list.
- `_targets.R:260` (`recent_data`) and `R/postprocess.R:86` (`default_year`):
  - Drive both from one `RECENT_YEAR` constant.
  - Otherwise every m1/m2 draw is silently stamped 21-22.
- `get_crdc_sch_data()` (`R/funs.R:1168`): map `SCH_JUST` → `JJ`.
- `crdc_lea_collapse()` (`R/funs.R:691-695`): make "latest year wins" for LEA names.
  Today, LEAs new in 23-24 get NA names.
- Year branches in `validate_le`, `incomplete_enrollments`, `validate_enrollments` and
  `sch_denom_enroll` (`R/funs.R` ~925-1150):
  - Route 23-24 like 21-22.
  - `incomplete_enrollments` currently sends unknown years to the 2015-16 code
    dictionary.
- `read.csv` (`_targets.R:184, 196`): add `colClasses` for `COMBOKEY` and `LEAID` as
  character.
- Download helpers and `test-download-paths.R`: add 23-24.
- Delete the commented NYC-drop lines in both templates.

**A2. NYC crosswalk: geographic districts, all four waves**
- **New `R/crosswalk.R`:**
  - `read_crdc_crosswalk(path, year)` reads Appendix K and returns COMBOKEY → 12-char
    `ncessch` plus `Matching Step`.
  - `build_crosswalk()` stacks 23-24 over 21-22, with the latest mapping winning per
    COMBOKEY.
  - Both workbooks are `format = "file"` targets.
- **`intersect_crdc_ccd()` (`R/funs.R:601-607`):**
  - Take the crosswalk as an argument.
  - Join CCD on `coalesce(crosswalk ncessch, COMBOKEY)`.
  - Waves 2017-18 and 2015-16 use the stacked crosswalk by COMBOKEY.
  - Carry the CCD school's `leaid`.
- **NYC rows only:** set `LEAID` to the CCD geographic-district `leaid`, and keep
  `crdc_leaid = "3620580"` as a column.
  - Geographic-district names and geography come from CCD through `district_dim`
    unchanged.
- **`district_dim` (`R/district_dim.R`):**
  - Add `parent_leaid`: `3620580` for the NYC geographic districts, NA elsewhere.
  - Add one row for `3620580`, named "New York City Public Schools", with
    `is_aggregate = TRUE`.
- **Log per wave:**
  - schools matched by COMBOKEY;
  - schools recovered by the crosswalk;
  - schools still unmatched. In 2023-24, 1,179 have no NCES ID: 164 NY, 131 CT, 97 MI.

**A3. `-5`/`-6` as missing (no imputation)**
- **Split `crdc_sub()`:**
  - Structural codes (`-9`) become 0, as now.
  - Missing codes (`-3`, `-5`, `-6`, `-7`, `-11`, `-13`) become NA.
  - `-10` and `X` are dropped.
  - Keep `crdc_sub_1516` in step.
- **`reshape_le` (`R/funs.R` ~1001-1020):** a school whose arrest cells are
  missing-coded keeps `ARRESTS = NA`, not 0, and the same holds for REFERRALS.
- **`crdc_lea_collapse()`:**
  - Aggregate reporting schools only, in both the numerator and the enrollment.
  - An LEA-year with no reporting school gets `ARRESTS = NA`.
  - `restrict_model_data()`'s existing `!is.na(ARRESTS)` filter then drops it from every
    model.
  - Carry `share_enroll_reported`. It costs one column, and the imputation design will
    want it.

**A4. Tests (written before each step's code)**
- **Reserve codes.**
  - `-9` under a zero ARRS-4 gives 0.
  - `-5`/`-6` give NA and are dropped from the model data.
  - `-10` X cells never reach the model data.
  - `SCH_JUST` maps to `JJ`.
- **Crosswalk.**
  - An NYC COMBOKEY lands under its geographic-district LEAID.
  - Non-NYC rows are untouched.
  - Unmatched schools are logged.
- **Collapse.** Partial missingness gives the reporting-schools rate and the correct
  `share_enroll_reported`.
- **Postprocess.** m1/m2 draws carry `RECENT_YEAR`.
- Update the existing download-path and release-string assertions.

**A5. Build prep targets and QA (0.5–1 day)**
- Run `tar_make(names = ...)` for everything upstream of the models: per-year targets,
  `combined_model_data`, `recent_data`, `three_year_data`, the `*_group` splits,
  `district_dim` and `stage_crdc`.
  - The exact name list goes into a new `scripts/run-prep.sh`, so the phase boundary is
    written down.
- **QA tables for you:**
  - by state and wave, 23-24 against 21-22: schools, schools with arrests, arrests,
    referrals, Black/white ratios. Flag moves above about 25%. Florida's SEA submitted
    centrally.
  - crosswalk match rates per wave, above all NYC;
  - `-5`/`-6` LEA-years dropped per wave, and partial LEAs with their
    `share_enroll_reported`.
- Turn `validate_le` into real assertions, with the 23-24 national totals.
- **Handoff:**
  - commit and push `_targets/meta/meta` along with the code;
  - wait for Nextcloud to finish syncing `_targets/objects`.

### Phase B: models and draws (euler)

**B1. euler setup (0.5 day, once)**
- euler has 32 cores and 256 GB RAM, and the repo is already cloned with ncdata links.
- It runs R 4.5.1 and cmdstan 2.36.0. efron runs R 4.6.1 / cmdstan 2.37.0, and
  `renv.lock` pins R 4.6.0.
- Upgrade R to 4.6.x, install cmdstan 2.37.0, run `renv::restore()`, then `git pull`.
- Run the dev-mode smoke run on euler (`scripts/smoke-pipeline.sh`), to prove the
  toolchain before the long run.
- **Session hazard.** euler's services are session-scoped, and a reboot leaves it dark
  until someone logs in at the console.
  - Run inside `tmux` under `caffeinate -dims`.
  - Defer macOS updates for the run.
  - RustDesk is the recovery path if it reboots.

**B2. Full run**
- `ssh euler 'zsh -lic "..."'`, then `tar_make()` in tmux. Prep targets are already
  current, so only the models onward rebuild.
- Set `CRDC_CORES` to the full 32-core box.
- Last full run: about 17.5 h to `posterior_db`, on a machine of unrecorded spec. Pooled
  data grows by about 33% from 23-24, plus NYC in two waves.
  - Estimate 15–30 h on euler.
  - Record the actual time in REPRODUCIBILITY.md.
- Review `stage_hmc_diagnostics`.
- **Sync load.** `export/` (a draws DB of 69 GB or more) and the model objects in
  `_targets/objects` sync through Nextcloud back to efron. Publish from euler to avoid
  waiting on it.

**B3. Citywide aggregate**
- In `build_api_db` (`R/build_api_artifacts.R`), sum the NYC geographic-district draws
  per draw, year, race, sex and model into `LEAID = 3620580` before summarizing.
- Do the same draw-wise sum used for `state_summary`, so the intervals are right rather
  than summed medians.
- Mark those rows `is_aggregate = TRUE`.

### Phase C: API vintages (efron, alongside Phase B)

- **Parameter.** `release` on every data endpoint: `2025.1`, `2026.1` or `latest`.
  - `latest` is the default.
  - An unknown value returns 400.
- **Loading (`entrypoint.sh`, `deploy/docker-compose.yml`):**
  - `RELEASES="2025.1,2026.1"` plus
    `DATA_URL_TEMPLATE=https://huggingface.co/datasets/civilytics/crdc-school-arrest-rates/resolve/civilytics-crdc-arrests-{release}/summary.duckdb`.
  - Fetch each into `/app/data/{release}.duckdb`. The `2025.1` tag exists on HF, and
    each DB is about 258 MB.
  - This replaces the single `DATA_URL` secret.
- **`api/R/db.R`:** a named list of read-only connections, plus `resolve_release()`.
- **`api/R/validate.R`:** `ALLOWED_YEAR` is read per release from each DB at startup,
  replacing the hard-coded list.
- **Model registry:**
  - `build_api_db` writes a `models` table, with "pooled" labels, into 2026.1.
  - 2025.1 falls back to the current static registry in `handlers_models.R`.
- **`handle_draws`:** pin the parquet URL to `@civilytics-crdc-arrests-{release}`.
- **Envelope.** Every response carries `meta.data_release`.
  - Estimate rows carry `is_aggregate`, and the `parent_leaid` of the NYC districts.
  - `/districts` returns `parent_leaid`.
- **New endpoint.** `GET /api/v1/releases` lists the releases, the default, the years in
  each, and the change notes.
- **Cache.**
  - A pinned `release=` keeps `s-maxage=31536000`.
  - Unpinned and `latest` responses drop to `s-maxage=86400`.
  - Purge Cloudflare once at launch.
- **Tests:**
  - `release` validation;
  - `latest` → 2026.1;
  - per-release year validation;
  - the draws URL contains the tag;
  - every envelope has `data_release`;
  - the NYC aggregate row exists.
- **Docs.** `plumber.R` param docs, `llms.txt`, `docs/api/*`.

## Out of scope

## Risks

- **Numbers move in earlier waves**, not only 2023-24. The vintage split and the change
  notes carry this.
- **NYC enters the panel in 2021-22.** The LEA random intercepts handle an unbalanced
  panel, but the pooled YEAR effect for 21-22 now includes NYC's contribution. QA should
  look at it.
- **Silent year mislabelling** if `RECENT_YEAR` is not the single source.
- **euler reboot mid-run.** Targets resumes where it stopped, but the run stalls until
  someone logs in.
- **Nextcloud sync** of a draws DB of 69 GB or more, plus the model objects.

## Release artifact

A post announcing 2026.1. It would cover:

- 2023-24 is in, with national arrests close to 2021-22;
- NYC is in for the first time, with district-level estimates inside the city;
- missing reports are no longer counted as zero.

The last two correct earlier numbers, and saying so openly is the point of a
continuously updated pipeline. Decide the post now, so the release doesn't sit
unpromoted.
